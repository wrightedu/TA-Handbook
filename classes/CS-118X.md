## Setting up new student due dates

This semester we are experimenting with a single lab page again. When a student gets added to the Pilot section, you will need to set up their due dates for the lab problems. This time *AND* date will vary depending on what lab section the student is enrolled in.

The due date is controlled via the "Special Access" feature of Pilot.
(Dropbox -> [Assignment Name] V -> Availability Dates & Conditions -> Manage Special Access)

From here, you can view and edit the due dates and availabilty of every students lab problems. Please ensure these dates are properly updated for their section.

| Lab Schedule              | Part A Due Time                         | Part B Due Time        |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Tuesday/Thursday Labs     | Tuesday at 6:05 pm                      | Saturday at 11:59 pm   |
| Monday/Wednesday Labs     | Section 05: 4:40 pm<br>Section 06: 12:20 pm<br>Section 08: 10:10 am | Friday at 11:59 pm     |


You also need to update the students attendance quizes.
(Dropbox -> [Quiz Name] V -> Availability Dates & Conditions -> Manage Special Access)
When updating those, please be sure to select "Allow only users with special access to see this quiz"
